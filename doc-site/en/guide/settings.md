# Settings

Settings are organized around the choices that govern work in a workspace: model providers and presets, enabled capability packs, change-review policy, Version History Beta, and Cottage Service.

## Models and capabilities

Add model providers and keys under **Models**, then choose the model that will handle the next request. Under **Capabilities**, enable only the tools the work needs. This limits the available tool surface and makes the conversation easier to review.

## Review policy and history

Choose how writes enter staged review and which sensitive actions require a decision. Version History Beta is configured per workspace and is off until you enable it.

## Cottage Service

Connect a reachable Cottage Service address only when you need enhanced search, webpage fetching, screenshots, or browser automation. Its connection state is visible in Settings; a failed connection does not silently replace your saved address.
