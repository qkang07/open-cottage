# Connect Cottage Service

Open **Settings → Cottage Service**, enter the HTTPS address of the service you intend to use, and connect. Its status should show whether the configured service is reachable.

Cottage Service is optional. Connect it when you need enhanced search, dynamic-page fetching, screenshots, or web automation; ordinary local workspace tasks continue to work without it. See [Cottage Service](/en/concepts/cottage-service-concepts) for its role and [deployment](/en/architecture/cottage-service-deploy) for operations.
